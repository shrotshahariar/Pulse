# Frequently asked questions

**Where is Pulse after I open it?**  
Look in the menu bar for the pulse icon or your selected readings. Click it to open or close the dashboard.

**Can I show just network speed?**  
Yes. In Settings → Menu bar, enable Network speeds and turn off the other readings. Choose upload, download, or both. You can switch between bits and bytes per second there.

**How do I see what is using memory?**  
Click the Memory usage card. Its detail view shows a history graph and the five largest measured apps.

**Why is part of my history empty?**  
Pulse records CPU and memory once a minute while running and retains up to 30 days locally. Gaps can appear when the app is closed or the Mac is asleep.

**Does Pulse send my readings anywhere?**  
Your monitoring history stays on your Mac. If you choose Show public IP, the app contacts ipify to look up your public address.

**How do I make it start automatically?**  
In Settings → Preferences, turn on Open at login. If macOS asks for approval, follow the prompt in Login Items.

**Is Pulse free?**  
Yes. The app is free to use and its source is private.
