# Mac Pulse
Mac Pulse puts disk usage, CPU load, memory usage, and upload and download speeds in your Mac’s menu bar. Click a reading to open the dashboard. Mac Pulse is free and works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

## Downloads
Public builds are distributed through [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/shrotshahariar/Mac-Pulse/releases). When a release is available, download the Mac Pulse app ZIP from its assets, unzip it, and move Mac Pulse to Applications.
