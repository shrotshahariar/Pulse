# Contact us
Pulse is developed by [@shrotshahariar](https://github.com/shrotshahariar).

For questions, feature requests, and bug reports, [open a Pulse issue](https://github.com/shrotshahariar/Pulse/issues/new). For bugs, include your Pulse version, macOS version, and the steps that produced the problem.

Issues are public. Crop IP addresses, account names, and personal files out of any screenshots.
