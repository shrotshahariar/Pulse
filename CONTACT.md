# Contact us
Mac Pulse is developed by [@shrotshahariar](https://github.com/shrotshahariar).

For questions, feature requests, and bug reports, [open a Mac Pulse issue](https://github.com/shrotshahariar/Mac-Pulse/issues/new). For bugs, include your Mac Pulse version, macOS version, and the steps that produced the problem.

Issues are public. Crop IP addresses, account names, and personal files out of any screenshots.
